===================================================================
  HUSTLE CASTLE BOT  --  Guide 2 of 5: YOUR OWN PICTURES (capture.bat)
===================================================================
  Almost all pictures come with the bot. Only YOUR name and YOUR
  fighters look different for everyone -- you take those yourself.

  HOW IT WORKS
  1. Start a dungeon by hand in the game.
  2. Double-click capture.bat.
  3. At the top it says which picture is next ("Picture 3 of 15")
     and where to find it in the game.
  4. Open that screen in the game, click "Take picture".
  5. Drag a frame around the spot with the mouse.
  6. Click "Save and next". Repeat until the bar is full.

  THE BUTTONS
     Take picture          photographs the game
     Save and next         cuts out your frame, goes on
     Skip                  leave this picture out
     < Back                redo the last one
     Picture from file...  use a saved screenshot instead

  REQUIRED (without them the dungeon does not start)
     Your player name        the name tag at the bottom of the
                             dungeon map
     Sacrifice fighter face  the fighter you give up first at the
                             sacrifice altar (squad screen)
  REQUIRED FOR THE ARENA
     Arena: your name        your name in your own shield in the
                             tournament pyramid (only visible in a
                             running tournament)
  OPTIONAL, BUT RECOMMENDED
     Fighter 1 to 6          face and name of your fighters, most
                             important first (revived first,
                             sacrificed last). Fewer than six: Skip.

  THE RULES FOR GOOD PICTURES
     - Frame tightly: for a name only the letters, for a fighter
       only head and shoulders.
     - Nothing that moves: no health bars, no numbers, no effects.
     - Wait until no animation runs.
     - Don't take fighters in the cathedral -- only in the dungeon.
     - A picture can be taken again any time; the old one is
       replaced.
