  HUSTLE CASTLE BOT  --  Guide 4 of 5: ARENA
===================================================================
  Before: the picture "Arena: your name" (Part 2).
  The bot looks at every opponent (power, sets, artifacts, emblems,
  alliance talents) and attacks the one with the best chance.
  Round 1 the weakest. It learns from every fight and marks
  players "safe", "avoid" or "never".
  A tournament is always played to the end.

     !notes                    notebook of all opponents
     !note Max 4 builders      your own note about a player
     !arenareserve 10          always keep 10 tickets
     !start arena 5 food       pay with food instead of tickets

  PUT PLAYERS ON A LIST YOURSELF (capture.bat)
  Lists: Doable, Safe win, Never attack, Avoid.
  a) "Take picture" of the arena pyramid, drag a tight frame around
     ONE white player name (not the clan), click "Arena: save name...",
     choose the list. Repeat for more names.
  b) Player not in your pyramid right now? Click "Arena: type names...",
     type the names separated by commas (e.g. Xenos, Walking Death)
     and choose the list. The bot cuts them out by itself as soon as
     it sees them in the arena.
