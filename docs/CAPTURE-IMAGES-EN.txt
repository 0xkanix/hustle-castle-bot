===================================================================
  CAPTURING YOUR OWN PICTURES
===================================================================

  IN SHORT

  1. Start Hustle Castle, begin a dungeon.
  2. Double-click capture.bat.
  3. At the top the window tells you which picture is next and
     where to find it. Open exactly that in the game.
  4. "Take picture" -> drag a frame with the mouse ->
     "Save and next".
  5. Repeat until the bar at the bottom is full.

  Done. This is one-off work -- unless you swap your fighters.


  ARENA LISTS (players you want to sort yourself)

  Lists: Doable, Safe win, Never attack, Avoid.
  - In the arena pyramid: "Take picture", drag a tight frame
    around ONE white player name (not the clan), click
    "Arena: save name..." and choose the list. Repeat for more.
  - Player not in your pyramid now: "Arena: type names...",
    type names separated by commas, choose the list. The bot cuts
    them out by itself as soon as it sees them in the arena.


===================================================================
  WHY AT ALL
===================================================================

  The bot is blind. It compares the screen with small saved picture
  snippets and clicks where it recognises one.

  Almost all these snippets come with the bot: buttons, symbols,
  digits, windows. They look the same for every player.

  Your fighters and your player name don't. You capture those
  yourself, otherwise the bot does not recognise your team.

  You do NOT need to rename anything in the game. You photograph
  your fighters as they are. How important each one is, you tell
  the bot by the order in the tool.


===================================================================
  WHAT YOU NEED TO CAPTURE
===================================================================

  REQUIRED -- without these two the dungeon does not start:

      Your player name          shown on the dungeon map
      Sacrifice fighter: face   the fighter you would give up first
                                at the sacrifice altar

  OPTIONAL, but strongly recommended:

      Sacrifice fighter: name   the name under that same fighter
      Fighter 1 to 6            your fighters by importance, each
                                with face and name

  Fighter 1 is your most important one. It is revived first,
  sacrificed last and goes up first in the duel. Fighter 2 is the
  second most important, and so on.

  Fewer than six fighters? Click "Skip" for the rest.

  The cathedral needs no pictures of your own. With fighters 1 to 4
  it revives fallen fighters, though -- without them it leaves
  them lying.

  FOR THE ARENA (only if you use !start arena) -- REQUIRED:

      Arena: your name          your name in your OWN shield of
                                the pyramid in a tournament

  Frame only the letters of your name -- without the clan below
  and without the frame. Without this picture the arena does not
  start. If the bot still cannot read your name, write it into the
  first line of a new text file arena-name.txt in the bot folder.


===================================================================
  WHERE TO FIND THE PICTURES
===================================================================

  All pictures come from the dungeon. Start a dungeon by hand once
  -- both screens are there:

  1. THE DUNGEON MAP
     At the bottom of the map there are two name tags, yours and
     your partner's. From here: "Your player name".

  2. THE SQUAD SCREEN BEFORE A BATTLE
     At the top it says "Drag and drop dwellers to form your squad",
     at the bottom your fighters stand in a row, each with face and
     name below. From here: all fighters.

  3. THE ARENA PYRAMID (only for the arena)
     Only exists in a running tournament: start a tournament by
     hand and wait for the ten shields (top right "Select the next
     opponent"). Find your own shield.
     From here: "Arena: your name".

  Please do NOT capture the fighters in the cathedral. The row
  looks similar there, but whether the names look the same is not
  certain -- and they are needed in the dungeon.


===================================================================
  STEP BY STEP
===================================================================

  1. Start Hustle Castle and go to one of the two screens above.

  2. In the folder C:\HustleBot double-click  capture.bat .
     Two windows open: a black one (it belongs to it, leave it
     open) and the tool "Capture pictures".

  3. At the top of the tool it says what is next, for example:

         Picture 3 of 15:   Fighter 1: face

     Below, in one sentence, what to frame and on which screen.

  4. Open exactly that screen in the game. Then click "Take
     picture" in the tool. Now you see your game in the window.

  5. Drag a frame around the spot with the mouse: press, drag,
     release. Wrong? Just drag again, the old frame disappears.

  6. Click "Save and next". The tool jumps to the next picture.

  7. Continue at 4 until the bar at the bottom is full.

  THE BUTTONS

      Take picture            photographs the game
      Save and next           cuts out the frame and goes on
      Skip                    leave this picture out
      < Back                  one picture back, e.g. to redo it
      Picture from file...    uses a saved screenshot instead
      Other picture...        for experts only

  A picture you already have shows a tick at the top. You can
  capture it again any time -- the old one is replaced.


===================================================================
  WHAT MATTERS
===================================================================

  FRAME TIGHTLY, BUT DON'T CUT ANYTHING OFF
      For the name only the letters, for the fighter only the face.
      Rule of thumb: whatever is inside the frame must ALWAYS look
      the same.

  NOTHING THAT MOVES
      No health bars, no changing numbers, no flashing effects.

  FOR THE FACE, TAKE THE HEAD
      Head and shoulders are enough. Armour and weapons change as
      soon as you re-equip.

  NOT DURING AN ANIMATION
      Wait until nothing moves any more, then "Take picture".

  ALWAYS THE SAME GAME SETTINGS
      Language English, HD graphics OFF, VSync OFF, window mode.
      Exactly as in GUIDE-1-SETUP-EN.txt step 2.


===================================================================
  PLAYING WITH A FRIEND  (optional)
===================================================================

  If you want to play the dungeon with a particular friend

      !start dungeon 10 lisa

  the bot needs ONE picture of their name. That is how it finds
  them in your friends list -- even further down, it scrolls itself.

  1. In the game go to the dungeon, "Participate", then "Friend"
     in the partner search. Now your friends list is shown.
  2. Open capture.bat and click "Skip" until the last step:
     "Name of a friend".
  3. "Take picture".
  4. Drag the frame ONLY around the letters of the name -- not the
     orange "i" behind it, not the clan below, not the number in
     front.
  5. "Save". The tool asks for the name. Type it as you will write
     it later in Discord, for example  Lisa .

  Once per friend -- as many friends as you like.


===================================================================
  CHECKING THAT IT WORKED
===================================================================

  Start the bot (run.bat) and type in Discord:

      !start dungeon 1

  If a required picture is still missing, the bot answers "Could
  not start" and names it, for example nameplate_me. Just catch up.


===================================================================
  OWN EVENT ITEMS (since 2.3, optional)
===================================================================

  If the bot should ALWAYS take something in the hammer event that
  it does not know yet (for example arena tickets), capture it:

  1. In the event, uncover a vase with a magnifier so the item in
     the glass vase can be seen.
  2. In capture.bat choose "Own event item 1" (up to 10).
  3. Frame ONLY the item in the vase -- not the glass rim.

  If the picture is not perfect, the bot cuts it again by itself
  during the event (file ..._auto). Nothing else to do.


===================================================================
  ARENA: YOUR OWN OPPONENT LISTS AND EMBLEMS (since 3.0 / 3.1, optional)
===================================================================

  How to put opponents into your lists (never, avoid, safe, doable,
  clans) is explained in templates\arena\gegner\LIESMICH.txt (bottom
  half in English): take a picture of the pyramid, frame the NAME in
  the shield, then "Other picture..." and type e.g. ar_sicher_1.

  New in 3.1:
  - No name twice in one list: capture.bat compares the picture
    with all lists. If the player is already in the same list, nothing
    is saved. If he is in another list, the window asks whether he
    should move -- the old picture then goes to templates-aussortiert
    (nothing is deleted).
  - After a safe or doable picture a window appears: player (as read,
    please check), your points against him (e.g. 215), the fight
    duration in seconds and whether he goes on the save-for-later list
    (then the bot attacks him only in stage 4-5). Leave everything
    empty = nothing is noted. The entries go to arena-punkte-erzielt.txt
    and arena-aufheben.txt in the bot folder.
  - Good emblems: in an opponent's unit window frame the round emblem
    under the word "Emblem" (only the circle, without the number below)
    and save it as ar_emblem_gut_1, ar_emblem_gut_2 ... Every unit
    with such an emblem gives the opponent +3 points.


===================================================================
